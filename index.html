关于我About Me

你好，我是杨博涵，来自海南比勒费尔德应用科学大学的计算机专业学生。我对机器人和自动驾驶方向充满兴趣，希望探索智能系统如何感知环境、做出决策，并在真实世界中行动。

Hi, I’m Bohan Yang, a Computer Science student at Hainan Bielefeld University of Applied Sciences. I am interested in robotics and autonomous driving, and how intelligent systems perceive, make decisions, and act in the physical world.

我曾在许继电气科学研究院参与宇树 G1 人形机器人的二次开发，也曾在深圳煜禾森林科技有限公司自动驾驶部门实习。这两段经历让我更希望将计算机知识与真实的机器人、智能驾驶场景结合起来。

I worked on secondary development of the Unitree G1 humanoid robot at XJ Electric Research Institute, and interned in the autonomous driving department at Shenzhen Yuhe Senlin Technology Co., Ltd. These experiences strengthened my interest in connecting computer science with real-world robotic and driving systems.
